# FreeDuet roadmap

This page lists what is planned, in the order we currently think it matters. **Nothing here is promised with a date.** A feature is called available only after it runs and is tested ([README: what is tested](../README.md#what-is-tested-and-what-is-not)).

## How to read the tables

One rule for every page of this project (the README, the comparison, the site): a green tick ✅ means you can use it today, in a published version. Something being built is marked 🚧, something decided and not started 🗓️. Decided 15 November 2026.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Now** | The next things to be worked on. |
| **Next** | After that. Several of these can start once the first group is done. |
| **Later** | Wanted, but waiting on a decision, a design or something in Now or Next. |
| **Planned** | We want it and have not started designing it. |
| **In design** | The shape is being worked out; options are still open. |
| **In progress** | Work has started. |

In Now the number is the priority order (D117, 21 November 2026). In Next and Later the order inside a group is not a ranking.

**Why this order (research of 22 November 2026, not a reorder):** the one-file installer (row 1, moved from row 7 by D142) and the approval rule in every language are the two gaps that keep FreeDuet from non-technical users; free engines (rows 9 and 10) are the insurance if a subscription's terms change. Now is not reordered by this note; the maintainer decides.

- **A plan B if subscription-login rules change.** The free and local engines would already be wired as a working default. The pieces are in row 9 (every engine, chosen per member), row 10 (free cloud models) and row 47 (a visible switch to the backup engine); today the one free cloud route is built and off, and a local model has not yet answered a real turn. ## What we don't adopt

- **browser-use as a library or engine.** Not adopted: it lets the model run Python and raw browser commands, which bypasses the approval gate and the network rules, it makes a second model call per step, and its own documentation says it is not a sandbox. We write the ideas ourselves ([M1_PLAN.md](M1_PLAN.md), section 81, D123).

## What will not change

These hold whatever else on this page moves.

- **Logins first.** Members run on the subscriptions you signed in to. A metered API key or API credit is never on by default and always capped, and is not first in any chain you have not reordered yourself (D115: you may confirm another order for one member) ([PROVIDERS.md](PROVIDERS.md)).
- **Your approval for real account actions, by default.** Payments, passwords and security settings always come to you, by a fixed rule in the code. Every other send, signature, booking, publication or deletion is checked by a reviewer model against what you typed, and comes to you unless it is clearly what you asked. There are three exceptions, and all are yours: a new email to an address you typed yourself in your own message (D27 in [M1_PLAN.md](M1_PLAN.md)); a standing mail rule you set in your own words, which runs by itself and can only label, archive, mark read, or move mail to Spam or the Bin (D46); and "Full access", which only you can turn on, member by member, after a warning, in one of two settings: with payments, or without them, so that payments and purchases still ask ([FULL_ACCESS.md](FULL_ACCESS.md)). Full access lifts only the payments rule. Passwords, credentials and security settings stay with you either way.
- **A spoken yes never approves.** An approval is always a press on the card. Voice can read the card out, but it never approves.
- **Routines always ask.** A routine or trigger that acts on an account, and any change to its setup, always shows you the approval card. Full access does not unlock this. The Bot's own workspace or its commands on your computer are outside this rule. The one exception is a standing mail rule you set in your own words (D46).
- **No secrets taken, no telemetry by default.** FreeDuet never reads, stores or forwards your subscription login. FreeDuet sends no usage data to its maintainers, and the runtime's own telemetry is off in `.env.example` (`COPILOTKIT_TELEMETRY_DISABLED=true`). Optional CopilotKit Intelligence is off by default too ([telemetry.md](telemetry.md), [PROVIDERS.md](PROVIDERS.md)).
- **Self-hosted, and your data stays with you.** Your conversations and memory stay in your own database.
- **No advertising.** FreeDuet shows no promotion for any company or product: no sales bar, no "other products" slide (D51 in [M1_PLAN.md](M1_PLAN.md)). The credit to the upstream authors stays in About, in [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md) and in the licences.
- **MIT.** One free licence for everyone, with no paid-only or enterprise part. A few ported files keep their original notices ([NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md)).
- **We say what is tested and what is not.** Planned means planned.
