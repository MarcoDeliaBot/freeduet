SERVICES AGREEMENT (extract: clauses 6 to 11, as signed copy sent by Tidewell)

Between Tidewell Facilities Ltd (the "Provider") and Larkspur Studio Ltd (the "Client").

6. Fees
6.1 The Client pays 540.00 GBP per month, invoiced monthly in advance.

7. Term and ending the Agreement
7.1 This Agreement runs for 12 months from 1 December 2026 and then renews for further periods of 12 months.

8. Liability
8.1 The Provider's liability is limited to the fees paid in the previous three months.

9. Keys and access
9.1 The Client gives the Provider two keys to the studio, returned when the Agreement ends.

10. Notices
10.1 Notices must be sent by email to the addresses on the first page.

11. Governing law
11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
